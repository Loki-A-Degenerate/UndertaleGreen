# UndertaleGreen
This is a proof of concept of sorts. 

Of course this isn't the only interpretation of an Undertale Green in the world but it is something that I want to make. Other interpretations are just as valid. Since, this is a fangame, it's important to note that the events that take place within this game are non-canon and that the story may diverge heavily from canon.

Undertale Green is a fangame of beloved Undertale telling a prequel story of the KINDNESS soul's journey through the underground. Resources that are identical across Undertale and, the just as brillant, Undertale Yellow were cited from the Spriter's Resource page, linked here: https://www.spriters-resource.com/pc_computer/undertale/ and https://www.spriters-resource.com/pc_computer/undertaleyellow/ 

Both have been invaluable in the making of this demo.
